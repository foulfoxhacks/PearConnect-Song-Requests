# Platform icons

TikTok, Twitch, Discord, YouTube, Instagram and Kick SVG paths are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), retrieved 6 September 2026 under CC0 1.0 Universal. Their fills were set to platform colors for the bundled brand preset. Monochrome and outline styles use CSS; no remote asset request is required.

The website globe is an original PearConnect icon (MIT). Brand names and marks identify the user's configured destinations and do not imply partnership or endorsement. Trademark rights remain with the respective owners.
